## Your pocket-sized control room

Cooler keeps the parts of Coolify you reach for every day close at hand. It is built for quick checks and focused actions when opening the full dashboard would slow you down.

- **See what matters**: View servers, applications, databases, services, and deployment targets in one place.
- **Act with confidence**: Start, stop, and restart resources. Cancel, redeploy, and roll back supported deployments.
- **Stay in the loop**: Follow live and past deployments, inspect logs, and get notified when important work finishes.
- **Keep sensitive data private**: Protect access and sensitive actions with biometrics, and hide private values when you need to.

## Built around your workflow

```text
Connect your Coolify instance  →  Check health  →  Follow deployments  →  Take action
```

- **Multiple instances:** Move between the environments you manage.
- **Operational overview:** Spot connection, resource, and deployment health at a glance.
- **Useful notifications:** Follow deployments, backups, scheduled tasks, and server alerts.
- **Native experience:** Get a focused React Native app with light, dark, and system themes.
- **Open source:** Inspect the code, shape the roadmap, and help make Cooler better.
